# R/D — retinal detachment drawing

**Live site: https://anthonyjohnob.github.io/R-D/**

Draw retinal detachments by voice, text or hand in the standard fundus colour code, with macular status,
Lincoff break analysis, a PubMed-referenced pathology library, an auto-written clinical note, and a summary
of published evidence on repair options.

- Runs entirely in the browser. Nothing you draw or type is uploaded; there are no accounts, analytics or cookies.
- Works offline after the first visit, and can be added to a phone or tablet home screen.

**Intended use:** education and documentation by eye-care professionals and trainees. Not medical advice.
R/D does not diagnose, treat or replace an examination, and it is not for patients making decisions about
their own care.
