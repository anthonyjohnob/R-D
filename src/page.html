      What you can say

      Detachments

      • RRD from 10 to 2 · RD between 9 and 3 · superotemporal RD
      • macula on · macula threatened · macula off fovea on · fovea splitting · macula off
      • bullous · shallow · chronic (adds a demarcation line) · total RD · subtotal RD sparing 5 to 7
      • extends to the arcades · posterior to the equator · extend it to 6

      Breaks and degenerations

      • HST at 12 · tears at 11, 12 and 1 · small tear at 10:30 · posterior tear at 2
      • giant retinal tear from 10 to 2 · dialysis from 4 to 6 · operculated hole at 11 · atrophic hole at 7
      • lattice from 7 to 9 · pigmented lattice with holes at 6 · paving stone at 6 · tuft at 3

      Complications and treatment

      • PVR C · PVR grade CP 3 · star fold at 5 · vitreous hemorrhage inferiorly · choroidal detachment
      • laser around the tear · laser from 3 to 6 · cryo around the break · encircling buckle · segmental buckle 10 to 2

      Any finding in the pathology library

      • cotton-wool spots at 1 and 2 · 3 flame hemorrhages superotemporal to the disc · drusen in the macula
      • BRVO superotemporal · CRVO · CNV involving the fovea · macular hole · ERM · disc hemorrhage at 7
      • choroidal nevus at 8 in the mid-periphery · PRP · silicone oil · gas bubble · exudative RD from 4 to 8
      • Places: at the fovea · parafoveal · in the macula · posterior pole · along the superior arcade · on the disc · peripapillary · mid-periphery · at the equator · at the ora · pars plana · in the vitreous

      Tumours and naevi

      • choroidal nevus 2 x 1.5 DD, 1 DD superotemporal to the disc, flat, drusen, no orange pigment, no SRF
      • melanoma 6 x 5 x 2.5 mm at 1 o’clock, dome shaped, orange pigment, subretinal fluid · 3 mm nevus at 7, minimally elevated, trace SRF
      • Size in disc diameters (2 DD, 2 x 1.5 DD) or mm (1 DD ≈ 1.5 mm); thickness 2.5 mm thick; distance margin to margin 2 DD from the disc · 1 DD temporal to the fovea.
      • Elevation: flat · minimally elevated · elevated · moderately elevated · markedly elevated · dome shaped · mushroom or collar button. Fluid: trace SRF · SRF · extensive SRF · no SRF. Say it again later to update: the nevus has orange pigment.
      • Drawn to scale against the disc. Elevation has no colour code, so R/D shows it as relief — shading, contour lines and a shadow that grow with the grade — and writes the size and thickness beside the lesion. Drusen are yellow stipple, subretinal fluid blue, orange pigment orange. Drawing by hand, drag a tumour’s edge to resize it.

      Vitreous and lens (for the repair options)

      • complete PVD · partial PVD · Weiss ring · no PVD · attached hyaloid
      • phakic · pseudophakic · PCIOL · s/p cataract surgery · aphakic · NS cataract

      Drawing by hand

      • Turn on Draw by hand, trace the detachment’s posterior border from the ora round to the ora, then swipe toward the detached side (or tap it). Clock hours and macular status are read from your drawing.
      • Drag tears, holes, lattice and the rest from the palette onto the fundus — or tap one, then tap the spot. Drag lattice, giant tears and dialyses along the fundus to set their extent.
      • Drag anything to move it, drag a span’s end to resize it, and drag it off the chart to remove it. Esc cancels; Delete removes the selected item.

      Teaching review

      • Residents: Send for review copies a link that carries the drawing. Fellows and attendings open it, correct the drawing with any tool, pin numbered comments with + Comment, add feedback, then Send back to resident.
      • The returned link marks every change — added, removed, moved — with the original border dashed underneath; switch between Corrected and Original, then keep either.

      Editing

      • undo · redo · clear · remove the tear at 12 · remove the drusen · move the nevus to 5 · right eye / OS
      • where is the break? or tear? shows the Lincoff zone

        Clinical basis
        1. Lincoff H, Gieser R. Finding the retinal hole. Arch Ophthalmol 1971;85:565–9. doi:10.1001/archopht.1971.00990050567007 — break-location rules 1–4.
        2. Machemer R, et al. An updated classification of retinal detachment with proliferative vitreoretinopathy. Am J Ophthalmol 1991;112:159–65. doi:10.1016/s0002-9394(14)76695-4 — PVR grades A–C, anterior/posterior, clock-hour extent.
        3. Lee R, et al. Long-term visual outcomes and the timing of surgical repair of fovea-splitting RRD. Retina 2022;42:244–9. doi:10.1097/IAE.0000000000003293 — fovea-on, fovea-splitting and fovea-off as separate categories.
        4. Berrocal MH, et al. Management of giant retinal tear detachments. J Ophthalmic Vis Res 2017;12:93–7. doi:10.4103/2008-322X.200158 — giant tear: full-thickness, circumferential, 90° (3 clock hours) or more.
        5. Jayanna S, Shanmugam PM. It is time for more colors! Indian J Ophthalmol 2022;70:3698–700. doi:10.4103/ijo.IJO_1004_22 — colour coding beyond the classic table (preretinal lesions in green).
        6. Pathology library — every entry links its own PubMed sources; all references were checked against PubMed records.
        7. CMS Local Coverage Determination L33567, Appendix A — fundus drawing colour code and chart landmarks (equator, ora serrata, ciliary processes). downloads.cms.gov
